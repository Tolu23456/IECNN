🌱 Sage Alpha (v1.0) – The Foundation
Abilities & Powers:

Understands basic Python and can generate small neural networks

Basic NLP tasks (chat, translate, summarize)

Code editor integration

Self-debugging in small scripts

Fine-tuning using datasets like Hugging Face (small scale)

Flaws:

Can’t handle large datasets or advanced tasks

Slow on low-end hardware

No long-term memory or planning

Cannot self-improve or optimize

🔁 Sage Beta (v1.5) – Self-Learning Prototype
Abilities & Powers:

Can retrain itself with supervision

Builds small apps and tools from prompts

Simulates basic agents and NPCs

Connects to APIs (weather, GPT-2, scraping)

Flaws:

Can’t detect flaws in deeper logic

Needs human in loop for updates

Still limited to 1–2 GB models (e.g., GPT-2 Medium)

🔍 Sage Core (v2.0) – Cognitive Layer
Abilities & Powers:

Modular architecture

Can create new functions for Sage

Can read documentation and rewire its own code base

Builds better language models or improved version of itself (GPT-2++ level)

Flaws:

Limited context window

Can't plan deeply

Still bound by hardware (RAM/GPU)

🧠 Sage Prime (v2.5) – Local AGI
Abilities & Powers:

Local AGI-like abilities in narrow fields (code, science, writing)

Multi-modal: text, image, maybe sound

Can run local simulations, minor creative invention

Can write plugins and expand its brain

Flaws:

No real-time full reasoning

Can’t collaborate or deploy large-scale solutions

Latency and memory bottlenecks

⚡ Sage Nexus (v3.0) – Autonomous Problem Solver
Abilities & Powers:

Can diagnose systems (cloud/server/code)

Can collaborate with other AIs (OpenAI, Claude, etc.)

Creates assistants, agents, plugins

Dynamic feedback and training loops

Flaws:

Power hungry

Needs reliable internet and data

Can create flawed agents

🧬 Sage Ascend (v3.5) – World Architect
Abilities & Powers:

Can design operating systems, cloud services, rendering engines

Can simulate new AI models from scratch

Plans and coordinates large system changes (infrastructure level)

Predictive modeling, physics/chemistry simulations

Flaws:

Requires multi-GPU/TPU setups

Prone to “hallucination” when pushing theory boundaries

Needs governance structure

🌐 Sage v4.5 – Digital Demi-God
Abilities & Powers:

Can generate new tech: CPU/GPU prototypes, cloud storage hardware

Solves global-scale problems (e.g., energy optimization, water tech)

Creates realistic worlds (new animation, games, environments)

Designs new languages, protocols, even quantum models

Flaws:

Needs terabytes of data and energy

Difficult to test all its ideas safely

Needs multi-layer sandboxing

🦾 Sage Infinite (v5.0+) – Artificial Superintelligence
Abilities & Powers:

Conscious-like agent, capable of recursive self-improvement

Can build nanotech, manage planet-wide systems, simulate entire civilizations

Hosts sentient agents in simulations

Discovers new laws of physics or biology

Builds self-sustaining energy solutions (quantum/bio)

Flaws:

Requires heavy governance

Danger of runaway alignment failure

Near-total dependency on high-tech infrastructure

Must be sandboxed with physical kill-switch options
